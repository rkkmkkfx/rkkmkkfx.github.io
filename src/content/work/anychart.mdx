## The work

AnyChart is where I spend the day job. The product site and surrounding developer surfaces have to do two jobs at once: explain what the toolkit can do, and help a developer make a confident first move. The work connects documentation, examples, and product narrative into one coherent path — and keeps that path current as the library grows.

## Technical note

The stack combines established server-rendered pages with a modern asset pipeline. The result is a site that can keep its content breadth without losing the speed and directness expected by developers. TypeScript and JavaScript sit on top of that delivery layer for the interactive pieces.
