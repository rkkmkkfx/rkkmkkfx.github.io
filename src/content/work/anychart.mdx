## The work

AnyChart is a product where the website has to do two jobs: explain the possibility of the toolkit and help a developer make a confident first move. The work connected documentation, examples, and product narrative into one coherent path.

## Technical note

The stack combined established server-rendered pages with a modern asset pipeline. The result was a site that could keep its content breadth without losing the speed and directness expected by developers.
